# Brief

## What we are writing

An adoption guide and a short migration checklist for teams bringing existing
documents under the standard.

## Who it is for

Team leads with an existing pile of documents and no appetite for a rewrite.

## Why now

A standard nobody adopts is a document about a standard. The blocking question
is not agreement; it is what to do with four hundred existing pages.

## The one thing they should take away

Adopt in two passes: declare ownership everywhere first, fix the writing second.
The first pass is mechanical and can happen in an afternoon.

## Constraints

- Channel: docs site
- Length: one guide, one checklist
- Deadline: unscheduled
- Must not say: that a full rewrite is required

## Done looks like

Two teams have adopted the standard and `atlas check` passes in their
repositories.
