# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be the colleague you would want reviewing your own draft.

## What we expect

- Critique the writing, never the writer. "This claim needs a number" is
  feedback; "you always overclaim" is not.
- Assume competence. Everyone here is capable and working with incomplete
  information, including you.
- Disagree with reasons. A rule you dislike can be changed by changing the file;
  say which reader is better served.
- Give the next person a route. If you block something, say what would unblock
  it.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or sustained disruption
of discussion.

## Reporting

Raise a concern with the editorial lead, or with any role holder named in
[`authority.yaml`](authority.yaml). Reports are handled discreetly, and the
person reporting is told what happened next.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in review, and anywhere someone is representing
this work.
